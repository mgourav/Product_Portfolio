# Project Name

One-line description of what this is and who it's for.

## Problem

What's broken, slow, or missing today? Who feels this pain, and why does it matter? Be specific — a vague problem statement is the fastest way to signal you haven't talked to anyone affected by it.

## Solution

What did you build? Describe it the way you'd pitch it to a stakeholder, not the way you'd describe it to another engineer. Link to the working artifact (demo, repo, screenshots) here.

## Tradeoffs

Every real decision has a cost. What did you choose *not* to do, and why? Examples:
- Scope you deliberately cut and what you'd build next with more time
- A simpler approach you picked over a "more correct" one, and the reasoning
- A dependency or tool choice you'd reconsider at scale

A project with no tradeoffs section usually means the tradeoffs weren't thought through, not that there weren't any.

## What I learned

What surprised you? What would you do differently starting over? This is the section that actually shows product thinking — the build is just the evidence.
